The Rebirth Collection

Our debut collection "The Rebirth Collection" was inspired by the Victorian and Elizabethan styles of fashion with a unique touch of contemporary style of affluence that appeals to the modern day eclectic style. The Rebirth Collection represents growth and a new beginning filled with hopes and beautiful dreams for the fashionably confident, resilient, and elegant woman. This collection sets a tone for what BÉMI IVORY stands for: timeless pieces that remain relevant season after season, and thoughtful craftsmanship.
