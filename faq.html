Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Your Questions, Our Answers

Every question deserves a thoughtful answer. Browse below, or write to us directly. We're always happy to assist.

FAQ

The Details, Answered

We've answered our most asked questions below. For anything else, our team is just an email away.

Orders & Payments

How do I place an order?
Simply browse our collections, select your preferred item, choose the size or variation if applicable, and click Add to Cart. When you are ready, proceed to checkout and follow the instructions to complete your order.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept secure online payment options depending on your location.
Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?
Orders can only be modified or cancelled within a short window after they are placed. Please contact our support team as soon as possible if you need assistance.
Will I receive an order confirmation?
Yes. Once your order is successfully placed, you will receive an email confirmation with your order details.

Shipping & Delivery

Where do you ship to?
BÉMI IVORY ships both locally and internationally.
How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location:

  • — Local orders: 2–5 business days after order has been processed.
  • — International orders: 7–10 business days after order has been processed.

Note: For Made to Measure, our production and delivery takes 14–20 working days.

How can I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email that allows you to follow your package's delivery process.
Are shipping fees included in the product price?
Shipping fees are calculated at checkout and depend on your delivery location.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?
We do not offer exchanges or refunds on our products. Please inspect your BÉMI IVORY product immediately upon receipt and notify us within 24 hours via email or WhatsApp for any defects.
Will the beads on a hand-beaded product fall off?
Each piece is thoughtfully and carefully designed. Minimal bead loss may occur over time with wear and handling. We recommend handling your BÉMI IVORY hand-beaded piece with care.
Are sale items refundable?
Sale or discounted items may be final sale and may not be eligible for return or exchange.

Products & Care

How do I care for my BÉMI IVORY products?
Care instructions vary by product. We recommend following the care label or instructions provided with your item to maintain its quality.
Will the colors look exactly like the photos?
We strive to represent our products as accurately as possible, but colors may appear slightly different depending on your screen settings.

Customer Support

How can I contact BÉMI IVORY?
You can reach us through the Contact Us page, email, or our social media channels.
What are your customer service hours?
Our support team typically responds during business hours and aims to reply within 24–48 hours.

Size Guide

How do I find my correct size?
Refer to our size chart and compare your measurements (bust, waist, and hips) with the measurements listed. If you are between sizes, we recommend filling our Custom Size Chart or contacting us directly.

Please note that custom orders are non-refundable or exchangeable.

How do I measure myself correctly?

Use a soft measuring tape (have someone assist you) and measure in inches:

  • — Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your bust.
  • — Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of your upper waistline.
  • — Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.
